About Study Planner

Study Planner is a simple tool that helps students keep their coursework organized.

Juggling several classes makes it easy to lose track of what is due and when. Study Planner gives every student a private space to list their courses, record assignments with due dates, and check off work as it is completed.

Your data stays private

Each account can only see and change its own courses and assignments. Passwords are stored using Django's built-in password hashing, and every form is protected against cross-site request forgery.

About the project
