Help

Register for an account, then add your courses from My Courses. Once you have a course, you can add assignments to it.

Click Mark complete next to the assignment. Click Mark incomplete to undo it.

An assignment is overdue when it is not completed and its due date has already passed.

Deleting a course permanently deletes all of its assignments as well. You will be asked to confirm before anything is deleted.

Each course code must be unique within your account so courses are easy to tell apart. Codes are saved in uppercase, so “inf601” and “INF601” are the same code.
